S.U.A.V.E.

Students United Activities & Venue Engine

S.U.A.V.E. is a web-based platform that helps student organizations propose events, reserve venues without schedule clashes, and get approvals in one place, from the first proposal to the post-event clearance.

What S.U.A.V.E. Does

Event Proposals

Submit proposals with the required PDF, preferred date, time and venue, then track their status as they move through review.

Venue Conflict Detection

Requests that overlap an existing booking for the same venue, date and time are flagged before they are submitted.

Master Calendar & QR Codes

Approved events appear on a shared calendar, and registration forms can be shared through generated QR codes.

Post-Event Compliance

Organizations submit venue cleanliness photos and an equipment return checklist, which OSAS and Admin verify.

How Approval Works

  1. A student organization submits an event proposal with its PDF document.
  2. OSAS reviews the proposal and approves or rejects it with feedback.
  3. Once OSAS approves, Admin gives the final approval.
  4. The approved event is added to the master calendar.
  5. After the event, the organization submits compliance, and OSAS or Admin verifies it.
